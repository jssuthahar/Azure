## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Instead of building a road from every town to every other town, you build one big **train station** in the middle. Every town connects to the station, and from there you can get anywhere.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

Virtual WAN uses Microsoft-managed **hubs** in each region. You connect **site-to-site VPN or SD-WAN branches**, **point-to-site users**, **ExpressRoute** circuits and **VNets** to the hubs, and Virtual WAN handles routing and transit between them, optionally with Azure Firewall in a **secured hub**.
