interview:
  - level: beginner
    q: What is the difference between authentication and authorization?
    a: >-
      Authentication checks who you are, like showing your ID. Authorization checks what you're allowed to do, like which rooms your ID card opens.
    followUps: []
  - level: intermediate
    q: Why is MFA so important?
    a: >-
      Passwords get phished, guessed or leaked. MFA adds a second factor, like an app approval or fingerprint, so a stolen password alone isn't enough. Microsoft reports that MFA blocks the vast majority of account-compromise attacks.
    followUps: []
  - level: experienced
    q: Which MFA methods would you recommend and why?
    a: >-
      Phishing-resistant methods first: FIDO2 security keys or passkeys, and Windows Hello for Business. Then the Microsoft Authenticator app with number matching. Avoid SMS and voice calls where possible because they can be intercepted or SIM-swapped.

## Start with a question

<Story question="A hacker has your password. Can they get into your account?">

Without MFA, yes. With MFA, they also need your phone or your fingerprint, and they don't have those.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

To open a secret clubhouse you need the **password** *and* the **special badge**. Knowing the password isn't enough on its own. That's MFA. And SSO is getting a wristband at a theme park: show it once and ride everything.

</Eli10>
