## Start with a question

<Story question="A bank moves 10 TB a night between its datacenter and Azure. Should that ride on the public internet?">

Probably not. It needs a **private highway**: ExpressRoute.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

VPN is driving on the **public motorway** in a locked car. ExpressRoute is having your **own private road** straight to Microsoft's door: no traffic jams, no strangers.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

ExpressRoute connects your network to Microsoft through a **connectivity provider** using models such as co-location at a cloud exchange, point-to-point Ethernet, any-to-any (IP VPN) networks, or **ExpressRoute Direct** straight into Microsoft's network.
