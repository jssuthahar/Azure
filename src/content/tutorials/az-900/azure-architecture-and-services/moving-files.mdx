    a: >-

## Start with a question

<Story question="How do you get thousands of files into Azure: typing, clicking or syncing?">

All three are possible: **AzCopy** for typing commands, **Storage Explorer** for clicking, and **Azure File Sync** for keeping a server in sync automatically.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

- **AzCopy** is telling a robot exactly what to copy.
- **Storage Explorer** is dragging files with your mouse.
- **File Sync** is a magic folder that always matches its twin in the cloud.

</Eli10>
