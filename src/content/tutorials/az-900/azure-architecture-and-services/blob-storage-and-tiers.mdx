## Start with a question

<Story question="Should last week’s photos and ten-year-old tax records cost the same to store?">

No. You look at recent photos every day; old records almost never. **Access tiers** let you pay less for data you rarely touch.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Your **school bag** (Hot) holds things you use daily. Your **bedroom cupboard** (Cool) holds things for this term. The **attic** (Cold) holds things for next year. The **storage unit across town** (Archive) is super cheap, but it takes ages to fetch things back.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

| Tier | Best for | Minimum duration | Availability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hot | Frequently accessed data | None | Online |
| Cool | Infrequently accessed | 30 days | Online |
| Cold | Rarely accessed | 90 days | Online |
| Archive | Long-term retention | 180 days | **Offline** (rehydrate first) |

Hot, Cool and Cold can be set as the account default; Archive is set per blob. **Lifecycle management** rules move blobs automatically.
