## Start with a question

<Story question="If a rule is set at the very top of your company’s Azure, how far down does it reach?">

All the way down. That is the power of the **management hierarchy**.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Think of **Russian nesting dolls**. The biggest doll (management group) holds a smaller doll (subscription), which holds a smaller one (resource group), which holds the tiniest dolls (resources). Paint a sticker on the big doll's rule book, and every doll inside follows it.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

| Level | Contains | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Management group | Subscriptions, other management groups | Company-wide policy and access |
| Subscription | Resource groups | Billing and access boundary |
| Resource group | Resources | Lifecycle grouping for an app or environment |
| Resource | – | The actual VM, database, storage account… |
