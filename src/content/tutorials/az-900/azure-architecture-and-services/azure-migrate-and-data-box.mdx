## Start with a question

<Story question="It would take 4 months to upload your data over the office internet. Is there a faster way?">

Yes. Put it in a box and ship it. That's **Azure Data Box**. And for planning the whole move, there's **Azure Migrate**.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Moving house? **Azure Migrate** is the moving company that visits first, makes a list of everything and plans the move. **Data Box** is the giant removal van for the heavy stuff that's too big to carry by hand.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

**Azure Migrate** provides integrated tools for discovery, assessment and migration of servers (VMware, Hyper-V, physical), databases (with Database Migration Service), web apps and VDI, plus a **business case** feature comparing on-premises and Azure costs.

**Azure Data Box** devices are encrypted, rugged and shipped by courier. Typical uses: one-time bulk migration, initial data seeding, periodic uploads of large data, and **exporting** data from Azure for disaster recovery or compliance.
