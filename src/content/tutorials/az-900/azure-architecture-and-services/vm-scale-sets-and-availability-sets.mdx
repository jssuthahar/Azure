    q: What are fault domains and update domains?
    a: >-
      A fault domain is a group of hardware that shares power and networking, like a rack. An update domain is a group of VMs that can be rebooted at the same time for maintenance. An availability set spreads VMs across up to three fault domains and multiple update domains, so failures and updates never hit all VMs at once.
    followUps: []
  - level: experienced
    q: Availability sets, availability zones or scale sets?
    a: >-
      Availability sets protect against rack and maintenance failures within one datacenter. Zones protect against a whole datacenter failing, with a higher SLA, and are preferred where available. Scale sets add elastic scaling and can themselves be spread across zones. For new workloads I would use a zone-spanning scale set.

## Start with a question

<Story question="How do you handle Black Friday traffic, and make sure one broken rack doesn’t take your shop offline?">

You need two things: **more servers when busy** (scale sets) and **servers spread out so one failure doesn't kill them all** (availability sets).

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

A **scale set** is like a bakery that hires more bakers when the queue gets long and sends them home when it's quiet.

An **availability set** is like not putting all your eggs in one basket: the bakers work in different kitchens, so one broken oven doesn't stop the bakery.

</Eli10>
