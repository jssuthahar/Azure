answer: >-
  Azure Storage always keeps multiple copies of your data. **LRS** keeps three copies in one datacenter. **ZRS** keeps three copies across three availability zones. **GRS** keeps three copies locally plus three in a secondary region. **GZRS** combines ZRS in the primary region with a copy in a secondary region. **RA-GRS** and **RA-GZRS** also let you read from the secondary region.

keyTerms:
  - term: LRS
    definition: Locally redundant storage. 3 copies in one datacenter.
  - term: ZRS
    definition: Zone-redundant storage. 3 copies across 3 availability zones.
  - term: GRS
    definition: Geo-redundant storage. LRS plus asynchronous copy to a secondary region.
  - term: GZRS
    definition: Geo-zone-redundant storage. ZRS plus asynchronous copy to a secondary region.
  - term: RA-
    definition: Read access to the secondary region at all times (RA-GRS, RA-GZRS).
interview:
  - level: beginner
    q: What is the difference between LRS and ZRS?
    a: >-
      LRS keeps three copies in one datacenter, protecting against disk or rack failures. ZRS spreads three copies across availability zones, so data survives a whole datacenter failure.
    followUps: []
  - level: intermediate
    q: When would you choose GZRS?
    a: >-
      When data must survive both a datacenter failure (zones) and a regional disaster (secondary region). It suits critical data with high availability needs. Add RA- if applications must read from the secondary during an outage.
    followUps: []
  - level: experienced
    q: What should you know about the secondary region copy?
    a: >-
      Replication to the secondary is asynchronous, so the latest writes may be lost in a disaster. The recovery point is not zero. Without RA-, the secondary is not readable until a failover. After a customer-initiated failover, the account becomes LRS in the new primary until you reconfigure it.
    followUps: []

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

You write your homework three times:

- **LRS**: all three copies in your bedroom.
- **ZRS**: one copy at home, one at gran's, one at school (same town).
- **GRS/GZRS**: plus extra copies at your cousin's in another city.

The more places, the safer your homework.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

| Option | Copies | Survives | Read secondary? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LRS | 3 in one datacenter | Disk and rack failures | – |
| ZRS | 3 across 3 zones | Datacenter failure | – |
| GRS | 3 local + 3 in secondary region | Regional disaster | RA-GRS |
| GZRS | 3 across zones + 3 in secondary region | Datacenter and regional disasters | RA-GZRS |
