## Start with a question

<Story question="A partner company needs access to one of your project apps. Do you give each of their people a new username and password?">

You don't have to. With **external identities** they sign in with the accounts they already have.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

When a friend from another school visits, they don't get a new student card. They show **their own school card**, and you give them a **visitor sticker** for the rooms they may enter.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

| Scenario | Feature | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Partners use your internal apps | B2B collaboration (guests) | Suppliers, consultants |
| Shared Teams channels with another company | B2B direct connect | Partner organisations |
| Customers sign up to your public app | Microsoft Entra External ID (customers) | Consumers |
