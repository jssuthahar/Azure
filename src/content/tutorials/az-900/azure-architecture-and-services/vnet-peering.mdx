## Start with a question

<Story question="Your app’s network is in West Europe and your data platform is in North Europe. How do they talk privately?">

You **peer** the two virtual networks. They connect over Microsoft's own private cables.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Two neighbours build a **gate in the fence** between their gardens. Now the kids can visit each other without going out onto the busy street. Peering is that private gate between two networks.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

Peering links two VNets so resources communicate with **private IPs**, low latency and high bandwidth. **Regional peering** joins VNets in one region; **global peering** joins VNets in different regions. Traffic stays on the **Microsoft backbone**. Peering is **non-transitive** and requires **non-overlapping** address spaces.
