## Start with a question

<Story question="A server costs 5,000 to buy. Is that what it really costs your company?">

Not even close. Add power, cooling, space, licences, backups and the people who run it. That's the **total cost of ownership**.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

A puppy isn't just the price you pay at the shop. There's food, the vet, toys and a bed. That's the **total cost** of owning a puppy. Servers are the same.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

The **TCO calculator** asks for your current servers, databases, storage and networking, plus assumptions like electricity cost and IT labour, then compares on-premises costs with the equivalent Azure resources over several years. **Azure Migrate's business case** goes further using real discovery data, cash-flow projections and savings from benefits like Azure Hybrid Benefit.
