keyTerms:
  - term: Policy definition
    definition: A rule describing a condition and an effect, such as "deny VMs outside allowed sizes".
  - term: Initiative
    definition: A group of related policy definitions assigned together.
  - term: Effect
    definition: What happens when a rule matches, such as Audit, Deny, Modify or DeployIfNotExists.
  - term: Remediation
    definition: Fixing existing non-compliant resources automatically.

## Start with a question

<Story question="How do you stop anyone in the company creating a resource in a forbidden country, even by accident?">

You write the rule once in **Azure Policy**, and Azure blocks it for everyone.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Azure Policy is a **robot playground monitor**. The rule is "no climbing the fence". The robot stops anyone who tries, writes down who broke rules before it arrived, and can even fix some problems by itself.

</Eli10>
