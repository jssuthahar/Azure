## Start with a question

<Story question="Half your servers are in Azure, a quarter are in your datacenter and the rest are in another cloud. Do you need three management tools?">

Not with **Azure Arc**. It brings all of them into one place: Azure.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Imagine a **universal TV remote** that controls the TV, the music player and the lights, even though they're made by different companies. Azure Arc is a universal remote for servers everywhere.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

Install the Arc agent (or connect a cluster), and the resource gets an Azure resource ID. From then on you can apply **Azure Policy**, enable **Defender for Cloud**, collect logs with **Azure Monitor**, patch with **Azure Update Manager** and manage access with **RBAC**, just like a native Azure resource.
