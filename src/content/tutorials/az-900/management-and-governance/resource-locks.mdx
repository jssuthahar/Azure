  **Resource locks** prevent resources from being **accidentally deleted or changed**. A **Delete** lock (CanNotDelete) allows changes but blocks deletion. ## Start with a question

<Story question="What stops a tired admin deleting the production database at 2 a.m.?">

A **resource lock**. Even with full permissions, the delete simply fails.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

A lock is like a **child-safety cap** on a medicine bottle. You can still see what's inside, but you can't open it by accident. To open it, you deliberately remove the cap first.

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

| Lock | Read | Modify | Delete |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delete (CanNotDelete) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| ReadOnly | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

Locks override RBAC: even an **Owner** must remove the lock before deleting or changing a locked resource.

## When to use it and when not to

<UseWhen use={['Production and shared resources that must never be deleted by accident.']} avoid={['ReadOnly locks on resources that need normal operations like scaling.']} />
