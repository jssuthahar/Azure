  **Azure Service Health** keeps you informed about the health of Azure itself. It has three parts. **Azure status** is a global view of the health of all Azure services in all regions. **Service Health** is a personalised view of incidents, planned maintenance and advisories that affect *your* services and regions. **Resource Health** shows the health of *your individual* resources, such as a specific VM.

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

- **Azure status** is the **national weather report**: is there a storm anywhere?
- **Service Health** is **your town's forecast**: will the storm hit you?
- **Resource Health** is looking **out of your own window**: is it raining on your house?

</Eli10>

## The technical explanation

| View | Scope | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Azure status | Global, public | Widespread incidents across Azure |
| Service Health | Your subscriptions | Service issues, planned maintenance, health and security advisories |
| Resource Health | Your resources | Current and past availability of each resource |
