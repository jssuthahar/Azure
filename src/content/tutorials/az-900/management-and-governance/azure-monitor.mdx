## Start with a question

<Story question="Your website slowed down at 9:14 p.m. How do you find out why, and how do you get woken up next time?">

**Azure Monitor** collects the evidence, **Log Analytics** helps you investigate it, and **alerts** wake you up next time.

</Story>

## Explain it like I'm 10

<Eli10>

Azure Monitor is like a **fitness tracker** for your computers: it counts heartbeats (metrics), keeps a diary (logs), and buzzes when something looks wrong (alerts). Application Insights is a fitness tracker just for your app.

</Eli10>

A sample KQL query in Log Analytics:

```kusto
AppRequests
| where TimeGenerated > ago(1h) and Success == false
| summarize failures = count() by Name
| order by failures desc
```
