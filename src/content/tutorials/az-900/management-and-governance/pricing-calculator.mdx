answer: >-
  The **Azure pricing calculator** is a free web tool that **estimates the cost of provisioning Azure resources before you deploy them**. You add services such as VMs, storage and databases, choose options like region, size, tier and hours, and it shows an estimated monthly cost you can save, share and export. It doesn't create any resources.

    q: How is the pricing calculator different from the TCO calculator?
    a: >-
      The pricing calculator estimates the cost of Azure services you plan to use. The TCO calculator compares the total cost of running workloads on-premises versus in Azure, including things like hardware, power and staff.

Then validate with a small pilot and Cost Management data.
