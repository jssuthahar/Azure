## Start with a question

<Story question="How do you stop your phone bill from shocking you at the end of the month?">

Most phone apps let you set a data warning: a text at 50% of your allowance, another at 80%. Some even tell you "at this rate you'll run out by the 20th". The warning doesn't cut you off, it just gives you time to switch to Wi-Fi. And now and then the app suggests a cheaper plan because you never use half of what you pay for.

In Azure the warnings are **budget alerts** (actual and forecasted), and the "cheaper plan" tip is **Azure Advisor**.

</Story>

## The technical explanation

### Budgets

Portal: **Cost Management → Budgets → + Add** (pick the scope first). Two tabs:

**Create a budget**
- **Scope**: management group, subscription, resource group (or a billing scope). **Filters** narrow it, for example *Tag: costcenter = FIN-042* or one resource type.
- **Budget details**: name, **reset period** (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually, or billing month/quarter/year), **creation date** and **expiration date**.
- **Budget amount**, with a suggested figure based on past spend.

**Set alerts**
- **Alert conditions**: **Type** (**Actual** or **Forecasted**), **% of budget**, and an optional **action group**.
- **Alert recipients (email)** and language.

```bash
az consumption budget create --budget-name fin-monthly --amount 500 \
  --category cost --time-grain monthly \
  --start-date 2026-12-01 --end-date 2027-11-30 --resource-group rg-finance
az consumption budget list -o table
```

```powershell
New-AzConsumptionBudget -Name fin-monthly -Amount 500 -Category Cost -TimeGrain Monthly `
  -StartDate 2026-12-01 -EndDate 2027-11-30 -ContactEmail finance-ops@contoso.com -NotificationKey a80 -NotificationThreshold 80 -NotificationEnabled
```

Budgets **don't stop** anything. To act, point an alert at an **action group** that runs an **Automation runbook**, **Logic App**, **Azure Function** or webhook, for example to deallocate dev VMs. Cost data is refreshed several times a day (up to about every 4 hours for most scopes), so alerts can lag real spending.

### Cost alerts

**Cost Management → Cost alerts** lists **budget** alerts, **credit** alerts (for prepaid commitments) and **department spending quota** alerts for EA, plus **anomaly** alerts, which you subscribe to from Cost analysis's smart views to get an email when daily spend jumps or drops unexpectedly.

### Azure Advisor

Advisor is free and studies your usage. Its **Cost** recommendations include:

- Right-size or shut down **underutilised VMs** (based on CPU and network over a lookback period you can configure).
- Buy **reservations** or **savings plans** for steady workloads.
- Delete unattached **public IP addresses** and idle resources.
- Use **lifecycle management** or cheaper tiers for storage.

Each recommendation shows impact, affected resources and estimated savings, and you can **postpone** or **dismiss** it. Advisor also covers **Security**, **Reliability**, **Operational excellence** and **Performance**, and an **Advisor score** shows progress. You can set up **Advisor alerts** and weekly **recommendation digests** to an action group.

```bash
az advisor recommendation list --category Cost -o table
az advisor configuration update --low-cpu-threshold 10    # VM utilisation rule
```

<Callout type="exam">
"Notify when cost reaches X%" = **budget** alert. "Before it happens" = **Forecasted**. "Automatically stop resources" = budget + **action group** + automation, because budgets alone never stop anything. "Recommend cheaper VM sizes" or "find idle resources" = **Azure Advisor**. A budget can be scoped by **tag** with a filter, which is how departments share a subscription.
</Callout>

<DeepDive title="Budgets at scale">

Budgets can be deployed with ARM, Bicep or the Consumption REST API, so a subscription-vending pipeline can create one for every new subscription. Management group budgets work for most offer types but not every billing setup; check the scope support for your agreement. For chargeback, combine **tag policies** (so the data is right), **cost allocation rules** (to split shared costs like a hub network) and scheduled **exports** to a storage account for Power BI.

</DeepDive>
