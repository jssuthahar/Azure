## Start with a question

<Story question="Where do you keep copies of your family photos so that one bad day can't wipe them all out?">

Three copies on one laptop protect you from a corrupt file, not from a stolen laptop. Copies on three devices in three rooms survive a burst pipe. A copy at your cousin's house in another city survives a fire on your street.

Azure Storage gives you the same choices: **LRS** (one building), **ZRS** (three buildings across **availability zones**), and **GRS/GZRS** (plus a copy in another region). **Object replication** is different again: it's you choosing to send certain albums to a friend abroad.

</Story>

## The technical explanation

### The four options

| Option | Copies | Survives | Secondary readable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LRS** | 3, one datacenter | Disk and rack failures | No |
| **ZRS** | 3, three zones | A zone or datacenter failure, stays writable | No |
| **GRS** | 3 local + 3 in paired region (async) | A regional disaster, after failover | Only with **RA-GRS** |
| **GZRS** | 3 across zones + 3 in paired region (async) | Zone failure and regional disaster | Only with **RA-GZRS** |

With read access, the secondary endpoint is `https://<account>-secondary.blob.core.windows.net`. Premium accounts support **LRS** and **ZRS** only. The **Archive** tier is supported on LRS, GRS and RA-GRS accounts, not on zone-redundant ones. Prices rise roughly in the order LRS, ZRS, GRS, GZRS: for 1 TB of Hot data, LRS is around $20 a month and GRS about double (illustrative).

### Changing redundancy

Portal: **Data management > Redundancy**.

- **LRS ↔ GRS / RA-GRS**: a simple update (`az storage account update --sku Standard_GRS`).
- **Adding or removing zones** (LRS ↔ ZRS, GRS ↔ GZRS): a **conversion** that runs in the background while the account stays online (`az storage account migration start --sku Standard_ZRS`). To go LRS to GZRS, change to GRS first and then convert.

### Failover

If the primary region is down, a **customer-managed failover** (Redundancy blade, **Prepare for failover**, or `az storage account failover`) promotes the secondary. Check **Last Sync Time** first: writes after it may not be in the secondary and are lost in an **unplanned** failover. Afterwards the account is LRS in the new primary until you turn geo-redundancy back on. A **planned** failover swaps the regions without data loss, useful for drills.

### Object replication

Object replication copies **block blobs** from a **source container** to a **destination container** in another storage account, in any region or subscription, asynchronously. It's for latency (data near users), separation (a copy in another account) and processing pipelines, not for disaster recovery of the whole account.

Rules to remember:

- **Blob versioning** on **both** accounts, and the **change feed** on the **source**.
- Block blobs only. Blobs in the **Archive** tier, snapshots and hierarchical namespace accounts aren't supported.
- A policy holds rules (container pairs) with optional **prefix filters** and a **minimum creation time** that decides whether existing blobs copy.
- A source account can replicate to at most **two** destination accounts.
- Cross-tenant replication is blocked unless **Allow cross-tenant replication** is on.

Portal: **Data management > Object replication > Create replication rules**.

```bash
az storage account blob-service-properties update -g rg-storage-prod -n stcontosoprod \
  --enable-versioning true --enable-change-feed true
az storage account blob-service-properties update -g rg-storage-prod -n streplicaweu \
  --enable-versioning true

# Created on the destination; then apply the same policy to the source
az storage account or-policy create -g rg-storage-prod --account-name streplicaweu \
  --source-account stcontosoprod --destination-account streplicaweu \
  --source-container photos --destination-container photos-replica \
  --min-creation-time 2026-11-01T00:00:00Z
```

```powershell
Set-AzStorageAccount -ResourceGroupName rg-storage-prod -Name stcontosoprod -SkuName Standard_GRS
Invoke-AzStorageAccountFailover -ResourceGroupName rg-storage-prod -Name stcontosoprod
```

<Callout type="exam">
Map the requirement to the cheapest option that meets it. "Disk or rack failures, lowest cost" is **LRS**; "zone or datacenter failure" is **ZRS**; "regional disaster" is **GRS**; "zone and region" is **GZRS**; "read during an outage" adds **RA-**. "Copy blobs to a second account" is **object replication**, which needs **versioning on both** and **change feed on the source**. A premium account can't be GRS.
</Callout>

<DeepDive title="RPO, SLA and what geo-replication doesn't do">

Geo-replication typically keeps the secondary within about 15 minutes of the primary, but there's no SLA on that number. Replication copies deletes and corruption too, so redundancy is not a backup. Pair it with soft delete, versioning, point-in-time restore or Azure Backup for blobs.

</DeepDive>
